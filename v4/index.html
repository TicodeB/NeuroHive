Chapter 01 · The old way

THE FOLDER

Tue 21:40 — The inspector is due Thursday. The folder comes down off the shelf.

Tue 22:15 — The allergen list is from two menus ago. Nobody wants to open it.

Tue 23:30 — Reconstructing three months of temperature checks from memory. Everyone knows it. Nobody says it.

Wed 01:10 — Still at the kitchen table. The till sheets don't match Tuesday either.

It isn't laziness. It's that the records were designed for the inspector — never for the person who has to keep them at midnight, after a 12-hour day on the floor.
