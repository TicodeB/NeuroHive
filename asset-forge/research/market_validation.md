# Market validation & sales channels — supporting evidence (Phase 1 add-on)

Commissioned mid-Phase-1 to answer two questions before we invest in building:
**(A)** is there evidence that digital assets like ours actually sell online, and
**(B)** *where* — which online spaces / community shops — would we sell them?
The final platform decision is locked in **Phase 8** with fees re-verified live;
this is the preliminary, evidence-backed view.

## B. Where to sell — the channel landscape

Three layers, used together:

1. **High-traffic marketplaces (discovery / top-of-funnel)** — buyers already
   searching: **Etsy** (largest template buyer intent), **Creative Market**
   (design-led), **Gumroad Discover**.
2. **Merchant-of-Record storefronts (own checkout, keeps margin, handles EU VAT)**
   — **Lemon Squeezy**, **Gumroad**, **Payhip**, **Ko-fi**.
3. **Niche community spaces (free demand discovery, not shops)** — where
   hospitality owners actually gather and ask "I wish I had a template for X":
   restaurant/publican **Facebook groups**, subreddits (**r/restaurateur**,
   **r/bartenders**, **r/smallbusiness**), and Irish trade channels (Fáilte
   Ireland / Restaurants Association communities). For the **SK/CZ market**:
   local hospitality Facebook groups and regional template sites — EU buyers can
   purchase through the global MoR platforms, so VAT is still handled for us.
   (Specific SK/CZ marketplaces to be confirmed live in Phase 8.)
   [Where to sell templates — Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/blog/best-marketplace-sell-digital-products/) ·
   [Niche template validation — Julia On Purpose](https://juliaonpurpose.com/profitable-niche-ideas-template-shop/)

## C. Platform benchmark — green tick = pro, red ✗ = con (most pros wins)

Per your decision rule. **EU-VAT handling is the heavily-weighted criterion**
(seller is in Ireland → digital-goods VAT must be handled *for* us). Facts below
were verified live (May 2026) and will be re-checked before the Phase 8 lock.

| Criterion | Gumroad | Lemon Squeezy | Payhip | Etsy | Ko-fi |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Handles **EU VAT** for the seller | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Full **Merchant-of-Record** (zero tax admin) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Free tier** / no monthly cost | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| **Low transaction fee** | ❌ (10% + $0.50) | ✅ (5% + $0.50) | ❌ (5% + processor on free) | ❌ (listing + 6.5% + ads) | ✅ (0% on free) |

### Result
**Tie at 7 pros: Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy.** Both clear the heavily-weighted
EU-VAT + full-MoR bar. **Tie-break on fee economics** (decisive for low-ticket
templates at €5–€30): Lemon Squeezy's all-in **5% + $0.50** beats Gumroad's
**10% + $0.50**.

- 🥇 **Primary (preliminary): Lemon Squeezy** — full MoR EU-VAT handling + lowest
  all-in fee → best margin per low-priced template, fully automatable solo.
- 🥈 **Fallback: Gumroad** — also full MoR, and adds **built-in Discover traffic**
  Lemon Squeezy lacks.
- 📣 **Discovery channel: Etsy** — despite higher fees and Google-Sheets delivery
  friction, its buyer intent is unmatched; use it as top-of-funnel and to read
  competitor sales/price signals.

> Final lock is **Phase 8** (re-verify all fees + VAT + Irish payout live, then
> commit one primary + one fallback per brief [11]).
