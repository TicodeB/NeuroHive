### Full description

**— English —**

**Be inspection-ready with one folder.**

When an Environmental Health Officer (EHO) walks in, you want to reach for **one file**, not a drawer of loose paper. This workbook gathers the records an Irish/EU food business is legally required to keep, pre-built with sample rows, dropdowns and automatic pass/fail flags. Fill the amber cells, print it or keep it on screen, and have your paperwork in order.

**The 7 statutory records in this pack:**

- **HACCP Food Safety Management System** — your written food-safety system *(Reg. (EC) 852/2004)*
- **Allergen Matrix & Menu Declaration** — all 14 statutory EU allergens mapped to every menu item *(Reg. (EU) 1169/2011)*
- **Temperature Monitoring Log** — fridge, freezer and cooking checks with auto Pass/CHECK flags *(852/2004 HACCP CCP)*
- **Cleaning & Sanitation Schedule** — daily/weekly/periodic tasks with a "Done /7" roll-up *(852/2004 GHP)*
- **Supplier & Delivery Traceability Log** — record goods-in and batches so you can trace "one step back" *(852/2004 / 178/2002)*
- **H&S Risk Assessment & Safety Statement** — workplace risk rating + safety statement *(Safety, Health & Welfare at Work Act 2005)*
- **Fire Safety Register & Checks Log** — extinguisher checks, drills and due-date flags *(Fire Services Acts 1981 & 2003)*

**Why it helps:**

- ✅ 7 statutory records in **one file** — the legally-forced compliance floor, in one place
- ✅ Smart allergen matrix (Y/T flags) + automatic temperature and fire-due alerts
- ✅ Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice — no subscription, no login
- ✅ English + Slovak throughout (parallel headers, instructions and guidance)
- ✅ Instant download — start using it today

This is a **template you adapt to your own premises** — it helps you be inspection-ready and keep your records in order. It is **not legal advice** and does not by itself guarantee passing an EHO/FSAI inspection.
