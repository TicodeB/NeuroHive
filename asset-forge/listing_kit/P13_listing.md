### Full description

**— English —**

**"Will I pass my audit?" — answer it in 15 minutes.**

The single most stressful question before any certification or inspection is *"are we actually ready?"* This workbook answers it. Set a status against each requirement, run a 20-question mock audit, and the spreadsheet auto-calculates your conformance %, a traffic-light verdict, and your highest-priority gaps — then points you to exactly what to fix.

**What's inside:**

- **Clause-by-clause Gap Analysis** — 26 requirements across 8 sections (the management-system spine shared by ISO 9001, ISO 22000, HACCP, BRCGS, IFS and FSSC 22000). Pick a status; the conformance % and priority fill in automatically.
- **Mock-Audit Self-Assessment** — 20 honest "could you show the auditor…?" questions that surface gaps before the auditor does.
- **Readiness Score & Verdict** — your overall readiness %, a 🟢/🟠/🔴 "will I pass?" verdict, a section-by-section breakdown, and live counts of your high- and medium-priority gaps.
- **Next Steps** — routes you to the matching ready-made kit that closes your specific gaps.

**Why it helps:**

- ✅ Turns a vague worry into a number and a prioritised action list
- ✅ Generic spine works whether you're heading for ISO, HACCP, BRCGS, IFS or FSSC
- ✅ Fully automatic scoring — no formulas to build, just fill the amber cells
- ✅ Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice — no subscription, no login
- ✅ English + Slovak throughout · instant download

This is an **audit-readiness self-assessment** that helps you prepare for a TÜV-style certification body auditing you to ISO / HACCP / BRCGS / IFS. It is a **template, not legal advice**, and does not by itself guarantee certification. Not affiliated with TÜV or any certification body.
