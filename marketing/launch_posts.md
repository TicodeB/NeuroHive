> For an EHO-style inspection the things that usually trip people up aren't exotic — they're the basics being out of date: the allergen list not covering all 14 declared allergens by dish, temperature logs with gaps and no signatures, training records that stop at whoever was hired in 2023, and a safety statement nobody has reviewed since it was written. If you fix those four before anything else you're most of the way there. Keep everything dated and signed — an inspector trusts a boring, current record far more than a beautiful stale one.
>
> Full disclosure: I run Leanta, a small Irish shop selling compliance workbooks, so I have skin in this game. There's a free 10-question self-check at leanta.ie/quiz.html if it helps — but the advice above stands whether or not you ever click it.
