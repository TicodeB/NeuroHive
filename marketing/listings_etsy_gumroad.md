## Etsy delivery note — applies to every Etsy listing

Etsy delivers **static files**. Therefore:

- **Never upload the raw full .xlsx to Etsy.** A static workbook freezes buyers on purchase-day versions, and the single-business licence forbids redistribution we couldn't then control.
- Upload a **delivery PDF** instead (one per SKU, e.g. `Leanta-P1-delivery.pdf`) containing: (1) the **watermarked demo** preview, (2) the buyer's **personal download link/claim code for the full, always-current workbook** (hosted on our store, kept updated), (3) quick-start steps, licence summary and support contact (hello@leanta.ie).
- State this plainly in the listing ("Instant download: a delivery PDF with your personal link to the full, always-current workbook") so no buyer is surprised.
