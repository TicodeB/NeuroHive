## 2. The moat — honestly framed

**An explicit reframe, stated openly because this document may be read by anyone:** the original ask was for "something obscured" so that customers "always need us". We are deliberately **not** building that, and here is why. Artificial dependency — locked file formats, crippled exports, withheld knowledge, manufactured complexity — is poison for a brand whose entire promise is *compliance and trust*, it runs against the spirit (and in places the letter) of EU consumer-protection law, and it produces resentful customers who leave the moment an alternative appears. Our customers can walk away any day with everything they bought: open formats (Excel/Google Sheets/LibreOffice), one-time licences, their data in their accounts. They stay because we keep being worth it. **The only thing that is legitimately obscured is our machinery — never the customer's exit.**

The durable barriers, in order of strength:

1. **Owned distribution.** The email list and the Skool community are relationships, not traffic. A competitor can copy a spreadsheet in an afternoon; they cannot copy two years of weekly office hours, answered questions and kept promises.
2. **The proprietary execution layer that never ships.** Internal prompt/agent playbooks, grant-application templates mapped to LEO scoring, per-vertical process libraries, the intelligence.db research base. Competitors see our *outputs* (workbooks, posts, courses); they never see the machinery that produces them at our speed and cost. This is the legitimate "obscured" part — trade secrets about *how we work*, not traps around *how customers leave*.
3. **The freshness treadmill.** Standards move — BRCGS Issue 10, the ISO 9001:2026 revision incoming, Irish guidance updates — and we ship updates to buyers **free**. A template-flipper who copied today's file must now run our treadmill forever or visibly rot. Most won't run it; that's the moat.
4. **Localisation depth.** Irish regulatory context + bilingual EN/SK inside every sheet + a founder physically on-site in Ireland. Too narrow a niche for big firms to bother with; too deep for template-flippers to fake.
5. **Grant-navigation expertise.** Knowing which LEO scheme funds which engagement (Digital for Business → Grow Digital Voucher up to €5,000 at 50%; Lean for Micro) and pre-packaging the paperwork is a sales advantage that compounds with every approved application — a track record competitors start at zero on. (Always with the caveat: confirm current terms with your LEO.)
6. **The success-story curriculum.** Only we have our own data, decisions and journey. The course (below) sells the *method as lived* — uncopyable without living it.

**What we never do:** lock-in pricing games, proprietary formats, fake urgency, dependency by obscurity. Stickiness comes from value: trained staff, current records, free updates, a community worth staying in.

## 3. NotebookLM edutech plan

**The source material** is `marketing/LEANTA_STORY_SOURCEBOOK.md` — the founding story structured as a teachable case study (origin → research rubric → product decisions → platform/money → launch week → lessons), each chapter ending with "What we'd teach" bullets and discussion questions, with a living metrics ledger.

**Workflow:**
1. Create a NotebookLM notebook; upload the **sourcebook**, the **launch runbook** (`LAUNCH_RUNBOOK.md`) and the **sales playbook** (`marketing/SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`).
2. Generate: **Audio Overview** (podcast-style episodes), **study guide**, **FAQ**, briefing docs. Generate in English first; then prompt for Slovak-language versions of each output (EN and SK both ship).
3. After every milestone (first sale, first grant approval, first agency client), update the sourcebook ledger and **regenerate** — the curriculum stays as honest as the business.

**Product forms, in sequence:**
- **Now — free mini-course as lead magnet:** "How a one-person Irish shop launched a compliance toolkit in a week" — 2–3 audio episodes + study guide, delivered to email subscribers. Cost: near zero. Job: feed the list and the community.
- **Later — paid "Zero to Launch" course (€49–99):** the method itself — the scoring rubric, the value ladder, Merchant-of-Record setup, Omnibus-safe marketing, grant navigation. Sold only once the metrics ledger has real numbers in it; the course sells the method and the journey, never fabricated results.
- **Ongoing — corporate training for agency clients:** staff onboarding on their own automated workflows (NotebookLM notebooks built per client, in their accounts). Recurring revenue, and **stickiness-by-value**: they stay because their people are trained and the system works — not because they're trapped.

## 4. Skool community plan

- **Free tier: "Irish SME Operators."** Weekly office hours (fixed slot, never missed), **template of the month** (a genuinely useful free sheet), **grant deadline alerts** (always with the confirm-with-your-LEO caveat).
- **Paid tier later (€19–29/mo):** the Zero to Launch course + a monthly group audit (bring your records, leave with a punch-list). Launch the paid tier only when the free tier proves demand.
- **Success metric: 50 members by end of month 1.** Track weekly; if short, the fix is more value posts and personal invites — never ads to a cold community.
- **The rule: the community gives value first and sells second.** Promotion at most one post in five; every promo post must contain something useful even to non-buyers.

## 6. CEE hub expansion gate

Slovak/CEE is the second market, not the second front. The bilingual EN/SK wedge already serves it from Ireland. We open a dedicated hub **only after all three gates are true**:

1. **€X MRR** — set X now and write it here so it can't drift: suggested **€2,000/month** in rolling toolkit + community revenue, held for 3 consecutive months.
2. **3 paying agency clients** (engagements delivered, invoices paid).
3. **LEO grant track record** — at least one grant-funded engagement completed end-to-end (application → delivery → paid out).

**Then, in order:**
- **leanta.sk storefront** — Slovak copy natively edited by a native speaker, never machine-translated and shipped; info@leanta.sk becomes a staffed mailbox, not just an alias.
- **Research Slovak equivalents of LEO supports** (e.g. national SME agency programmes) — verify every scheme before citing it anywhere; the confirm-with-the-agency caveat travels with us.
- **Partner recruitment:** 1–2 local partners (accountant, food-safety consultant) on the same referral terms as Ireland (20% or reciprocal).

Until the gates are met, every euro and hour goes into the Irish flywheel above.
