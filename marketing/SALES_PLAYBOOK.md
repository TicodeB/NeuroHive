- **Who:** SMEs within driving radius (on-site presence is the differentiator): cafés/restaurants, hotels/B&Bs, retail & petrol forecourts, food manufacturers, wholesalers, transport/logistics, builders/trades, farmers.

- **Lead offer:** the **free process-audit call** — map one painful routine process, show the before/after, quote the engagement.
- **The grant angle** (verified 09/06/2026 — **always tell the client to confirm current terms with their LEO**):
  - **Digital for Business**: fully-funded LEO consultancy project; completing one within the last 2 years is the prerequisite for the voucher below.

## 4. Sales scripts

### Walk-in: the 60-second pitch
> "Morning — Samuel, I'm local, I run Leanta. Quick question: if an inspector walked in tomorrow, would your paperwork pass? *(pause — let them answer)* Most places have the folder, but it's out of date — the allergen list, the temp logs, the training records. I make ready-to-use workbooks for exactly that. This is the café pack on my phone: HACCP records, the 14-allergen matrix, temp logs, cleaning, traceability, safety statement, fire register. €34 once — no subscription — works in Excel or Google Sheets, English and Slovak inside. There's also a free 2-minute self-check — scan this poster. And if you'd rather someone just sorted it with you, I do that too; the first call costs nothing. Will I send you the demo or the link?"

### Phone/DM: the 4-liner
1. "Hi [name] — saw [specific, true detail about their business]."
2. "I make audit-ready compliance workbooks for Irish cafés and restaurants — 7 statutory records in one file, €34 once."
3. "Free 2-minute self-check if useful: leanta.ie/quiz.html — tells you exactly where you stand."
4. "If it's not for you, no bother — reply 'stop' and I won't write again."

### Objection handling
- **"I already have a folder."** → "Great — when was it last updated? Does the allergen list cover all 14? Are the temp logs signed daily? The €29 readiness check finds the gaps in an afternoon; if there are none, you've bought peace of mind."
- **"No time."** → "That's exactly the point. It's pre-built — you fill in your details, not design spreadsheets. Most buyers have the first records live the same evening."
- **"Is this legal advice?"** → "No, and I'll never pretend it is. These are working templates built to current published requirements; you adapt them to your premises and you stay responsible. What they replace is the blank page, not your accountability."
- **"Why pay when free templates exist?"** → Four honest reasons: **freshness** (when standards change, updates are free — free downloads rot), **logic** (live formulas, flags, RAG dashboards — not flat PDFs), **bilingual** (EN/SK on every sheet), **one system** (seven matched records, not seven mismatched downloads).

## 5. Pricing experiments

- **P13 price test** (per §41 of the pricing research): test **€19 / €29 / €39**.
  - Launch at €29 (the §41 resolution). Run each band for a fixed window (suggest 2 weeks or ≥100 product-page visits, whichever is later) — one band at a time, same traffic sources.
  - Track per band: product-page visits → sales conversion, and revenue per 100 visitors. Decide on revenue, not conversion alone.
  - **Hold the ceiling:** never sell below the test floor (€19); no flash discounts.
- **Omnibus rules (non-negotiable):** no countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no "was €X" strikethroughs unless that price genuinely applied for the prior 30 days. Change list prices properly. Bundle saving stays sum-of-parts: €34 + €49 = €83 → €69, **saving €14** — that's the entire discount story.
- Everything else holds price for the first month. One variable at a time.
