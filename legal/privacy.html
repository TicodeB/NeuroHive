The short version

This site is deliberately boring about your data:

  • No cookies. No analytics. No trackers. No ad pixels.
  • The free readiness check runs entirely in your browser — your answers are never transmitted or stored by us.
  • We receive personal data only when you email us (your address and what you write) or when you buy (the checkout platform shares your order details so we can deliver and support the product).

Processors & hosting

The site is static and hosted on Vercel Inc. (which, like any web host, sees standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and delivery). Payments and VAT are handled by the checkout platform shown at purchase time (e.g. Lemon Squeezy / Stripe / Gumroad), which acts under its own privacy policy. Email runs on Google Workspace/Gmail.

Your rights

You have the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Email us and we'll act on it. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
