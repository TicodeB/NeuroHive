Would you pass an inspection tomorrow?

Answer honestly — Yes only if you could put the record in an inspector’s hands today. Ten questions, scored the same way the full Readiness Check workbook scores you (🟢 ≥85% · 🟠 60–84% · 🔴 <60%).

100% private: this page sends nothing anywhere. Your answers never leave your browser.

1. Do you have a written, up-to-date HACCP / food-safety plan that matches your current menu and process?

2. Could you show today’s (or the most recent) temperature records for every fridge, freezer and hot-hold unit?

3. Is there a 14-allergen matrix covering every item you serve, updated when the menu changed last?

4. Do you run a cleaning & sanitation schedule with sign-offs that are actually completed?

5. Could you trace any ingredient back to its supplier and delivery within 10 minutes?

6. Do you have a written H&S risk assessment / safety statement reviewed in the last 12 months?

7. Is your fire register current — alarms, extinguishers, emergency lighting and exits checked and logged on schedule?

8. Does every staff member have dated training records (induction, hygiene, allergens, manual handling) before working the floor?

9. When something goes wrong (failed temp, pest sighting, complaint), do you log it and record the corrective action taken?

10. If an inspector walked in right now, could you hand over all of the above within 15 minutes?
