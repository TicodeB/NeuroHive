About Leanta

Built from the floor, not from a template shop

Leanta toolkits come out of real production and hospitality operations experience in Ireland — the records inspectors actually ask for and the numbers owners actually need, mapped against EU and Irish regulation and checked against how inspections really run. Each workbook is researched per-vertical (hospitality first, food manufacturing and trades next) instead of being one generic template with the logo swapped.

Questions before buying? Email samuel.vyhnanek+leanta@gmail.com — you’ll get an answer from the person who built the sheets.
