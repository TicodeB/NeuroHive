Show, don't tell

The old way vs. the Leanta way

We don't ask you to imagine it. Every demo workbook is free, and on an agency visit we rebuild one of your processes in front of you — old way and new way, side by side, before you pay for anything.

The old way

Paper folders, blank pages, retyping

  • The compliance folder gets rebuilt in a panic the week the inspector is due.
  • The allergen list was made once, two menus ago, and nobody wants to open it.
  • Rosters start from a blank page every week; till figures are retyped at midnight.
  • Quotes and invoices written from scratch — so invoicing slips, and cash slips with it.
  • You find out the margin moved when the accountant tells you, months later.
The Leanta way

Same records, built once, kept alive

  • Fridge temps: type two numbers, out-of-range cells flag themselves. ~90 seconds.
  • One delivery line in the traceability sheet: supplier, batch, date. ~60 seconds.
  • The roster carries forward; labour-% turns red before the weekend does.
  • Dashboards inside the workbook tell you the number and the next action.
  • When a regulation amends, your file gets a free update — it never rots.

Why a workbook, not a BI platform?

The dashboard is already inside

Power BI, Tableau and FP&A platforms are superb tools — for companies with analysts to run them. An owner-operator needs six numbers and zero setup. Leanta workbooks put BI-grade clarity — traffic-light verdicts, live roll-ups, threshold alerts — inside the spreadsheet you already own.

Our dashboard rule: insight, not decoration. Every tile answers one question and prescribes the next action. If a chart doesn't change what you do on Monday, it doesn't earn a place on the sheet.
