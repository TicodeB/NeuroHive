The Leanta grant pathway

Start for €200 out of pocket.

You pay €200 to mobilise. It covers the on-site process audit and the complete, application-ready grant pack for your Local Enterprise Office — researched, written, costed; your side is roughly a 20-minute signing exercise. Then:

  • Grant approved → the project proceeds and invoicing is structured around the grant: the funded share is covered by the scheme, and your €200 is credited against any balance.
  • Grant not approved → you decide, with no obligation: proceed at the quoted price, or stop — and you keep the audit findings and the application pack either way. The €200 bought real work, not a promise.

Plainly: funding decisions are made by your LEO, never by us, and we will not promise approval. What we promise is a fundable, properly-scoped application and a project worth funding. Scheme terms change — we verify them at application time.
