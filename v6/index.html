Floor 02 · Trades people

THE INVOICE FINDS OUT LAST

The van knows the job is done the second the tools go back in. The invoice finds out three weeks later — if the docket survived the dashboard.

  • Quote → job → invoice on one thread, raised the same week
  • Materials & hours logged once, on the phone, on site
  • RCT, VAT and the safety file kept as you go, not in a panic

Floor 03 · Manufacturing

A FORMALITY
— OR A FRIGHT

Batch traceability, CCP monitoring, labels matching the current recipe. Your buyers will audit these. The difference between a formality and a fright is whether the records kept themselves all year.

  • One-line batch traceability — both directions
  • HACCP with CCP records an auditor can follow
  • Allergen declarations tied to the live recipe sheet

Floor 04 · Farmers · land & livestock

EVERY FIELD
REMEMBERS

Every field remembers what went on it and when. The notebook in the tractor doesn't. Then the inspection letter comes and the remembering all has to happen at once.

  • Spreading, dosing and movement logs that satisfy cross-compliance
  • Herd / flock register kept on the phone at the gate
  • Nitrates & scheme paperwork ready before the deadline, not after

One missed record can hold a whole payment. Kept as you go, it never becomes a scramble.

THE ONE THAT
DIDN'T COME BACK

Thirty permission slips went home. The term runs on chasing the one that didn't. Roll, returns, bookings and consents live in four places, and September never quite stops.

  • Consents & returns tracked in one list, chased automatically
  • Bookings, rooms and rosters that don't double-book
  • Records ready for the board and the Department in minutes

The real cost is teacher time: hours of chasing that should have been hours of teaching.

Floor 06 · Doctors & dentists

THE SAME QUESTION,
FORTY TIMES

Reception answers the same question forty times a week, the recall list lives in someone's head, and the calm waiting room is held together by a person who never gets to sit down.

  • Recalls & reminders that go out on their own
  • Intake & consent captured before the chair, not during it
  • GDPR-grade records — kept tight, shared only as needed

A calmer front desk is fewer no-shows and a receptionist who can breathe — both show up in the day's takings.
