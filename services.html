The method

Lean Six Sigma, applied to a real Tuesday

Lean Six Sigma isn't a poster on a factory wall — it's a discipline for finding where time and money leak, fixing the leak, and proving the fix with numbers. We run every engagement through the same five steps, and we measure before and after, so the improvement is a figure on a page, not a feeling.

Define & Measure

We stand where the work happens and time it. "Invoicing takes 4.5 hours a week and lags 9 days" beats "admin is a nightmare" — because you can't improve what you haven't measured.

Analyse & Improve

Find the actual bottleneck (it's rarely where it feels), remove the waste — retyping, searching, chasing — then automate what remains. Lean first, then AI: automating a wasteful process just makes waste faster.

Control

The step everyone skips. Dashboards with thresholds, owners for each number, a monthly review — so the fix still holds when staff change and menus change. This is why our work doesn't rot.

And the dashboards we leave behind follow one rule: insight, not decoration. Each tile answers a question and prescribes an action — see a live example of the workbook dashboards. If a chart doesn't change what you do on Monday, we delete it.
